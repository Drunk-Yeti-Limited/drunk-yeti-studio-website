The studio, in a few words

Drunk Yeti Studio is an independent game, software, and multimedia studio led by Michael Lambert Jr, who creates as 3ichael 7ambert. Its work spans original games, creative applications, illustrated stories, music, and the Drunk Yeti Desktop Buddy. The studio’s guiding idea is “Design with a Technical Mind.”
