Neverland Seafoods, Bristol Bay

Dillingham

During the first big salmon run, a new worker learns the pace of a Bristol Bay cannery from the people beside him. The line, the separate dorms, and Sunday dinner in the mess hall give the crew three very different places to catch their breath.

Beaver The newcomer
Teal chest and broad tail. Eager to take on his first big run, even when he needs help finding his rhythm.
John The patient teacher
Pigtails and black gloves. He knows how the line moves and makes time to show Beaver the ropes.
Bear The steady hand
Brown fur and a big thermos. He brings a calm presence to a shift that can turn busy in a moment.

A Place for the Small Things

Riggins

Riggins and the Meepmops share a clay-built world where the small details carry the story. A familiar morning routine makes room for company, and the closing pages linger on the pleasure of staying together one minute longer.

Riggins The quiet center
The story introduces Riggins through an ordinary morning. Coffee and unhurried gestures set the pace.
The Meepmops Small companions
Their small scale changes how a room feels. Their company gives Riggins a reason to pause.

One river, two valleys

Moly, Bart & Skid

When the village spring stops flowing, the trail leads to another valley and an ancient machine under the mountain. The river and its machinery give each member of the trio a different way to investigate the same problem. Created by Matt Mateo VII.

Moly Across the gap
Flips across chasms. Her field journal shows how she turns the expedition’s mistakes into practical lessons.
Bart Above the trail
Flies over obstacles. His recipe for soup for three leaves room for whoever else arrives.
Skid Below the surface
Swims where the others cannot, giving the team a route beneath the water.

The Long Way to Lunch

Drunk Yeti

An ordinary lunch grows into an adventure through a riverside neighborhood. A borrowed bridge, a storm, and a gull with an eye for unattended belongings keep the neighbors crossing paths.

Yeti The would-be lunch host
The studio mascot sets out for a quiet meal. The neighborhood has plans of its own.
Beaver The neighbor with a plan
Brings a practical suggestion when the bridge needs attention.
Fox The undecided guest
An invitation draws Fox into a day that takes a turn with the weather.
Gull The uninvited opportunist
Treats a quiet lunch as a chance to carry something off.

A city on a desktop

PenPals

An unaddressed letter turns a desk into an adventure map. Drawers, scraps, and narrow spaces become places to explore, and the friends’ different ways of making marks shape how they solve problems.

Yellow Pencil The optimist
Sketches possibilities before all the details are settled.
Red Test Pencil The careful editor
Blunt and precise, with a tender side that comes out when someone needs room.
Blue Pencil The observer
Spots small traces others pass by.
Pen The practical friend
Gives wandering ideas a shape the group can work with.
Crayon The improviser
Brings big energy, jokes, and unexpected ways forward.
Stub The tiny explorer
Can reach places the other friends cannot fit into.
