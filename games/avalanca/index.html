Avalanca: Downhill Riot

Explore Avalanca: Downhill Riot, a cel-shaded downhill arcade game with drifting, tricks, multiple rides, and a browser edition.

Find your line. Make some trouble.

Carve downhill from a bird’s-eye view, drift around trouble, and turn a good line into a better run. Avalanca mixes cel-shaded courses with tricks, side punches, and rides that go beyond a snowboard.

Avalanca in your browser

The browser build opens on its own game server and loads game files before starting. Browser content and controls may differ from the downloadable editions.

Pick your ride

The downloadable game brings snowboards, bicycles, jet skis, and more into its downhill playground. Different rides give the adventure a different feel.

Chase the next run

Race, improve a time, or explore in Free Ride. The full downloadable edition includes its existing destinations, modes, and Tours.

Share the descent

Local split-screen is part of the full downloadable edition. Visit the release page for its current mode and platform details before choosing a build.

Choose your edition

Lite includes solo Tundra Race, Time Attack, and Free Ride with Snowboard and Custom Rider/Odonis. Full includes its existing content and local multiplayer offline. Available builds and Frostpass access are described on the official release page.
