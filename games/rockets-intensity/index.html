A Rocket’s Intensity

Choose your world and mission in the browser hangar, or use Web Arena for the direct-connect experience. This hosted build is a release candidate.

Choose a mission

The browser hangar offers missions including Convoy Escort, Dogfight, Survival, Rift Race, and Free Flight. Pick an objective and difficulty before launching a sortie.

Make the flight your own

Choose from the art styles and airframes shown in the hangar. Adjust weather and effect density in the atmosphere controls, and use the flight manual to learn the available actions.

Find your controls

The game presents keyboard, mouse, gamepad, and touch controls. Its hangar includes solo and two-pilot split-screen choices; usable controls depend on your device and connected controllers.

Choose your edition

The hosted game identifies itself as a release candidate. The browser experience includes an arcade-flight hangar and a direct-connect web arena. Steam multiplayer is a separate path; a browser link does not establish Steam connectivity or feature parity with a desktop build.
