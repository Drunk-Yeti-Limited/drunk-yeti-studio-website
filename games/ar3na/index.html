AR3NA: All-Stars

Discover AR3NA: All-Stars, Drunk Yeti’s party arena game, and jump into the separate Open Arena browser experience.

Magic. Mayhem. Match point.

A well-timed strike can score a goal, steal a flag, or send a rival off the court. AR3NA brings original characters, colorful courts, and an appetite for chaos to a 3D party arena.

Official release & downloads ↗

AR3NA Open Arena

Open Arena is a separate browser experience set in the Concourse. It is not the complete downloadable All-Stars edition. Choose a guest name and enter from its own start screen.

Play in browser ↗ (opens a new tab)

A different kind of match

Magic Soccer, Stock Battle, Capture the Flag, King of the Hill, Tag, Sumo, and Orb Rush put different objectives on the court. Training and a character maker give you room to find your style.

Bring the room together

The downloadable game includes local multiplayer, computer opponents, and keyboard or controller play. Available player slots depend on the mode, connected controllers, and platform.

Explore beyond the court

Adventure chapters, asteroid hopping, survival challenges, and a physics sandbox expand the downloadable game beyond its classic matches.

Choose your edition

The Lite download starts with Magic Soccer and Sumo on Neon Stadium, training, and the character maker. The separate Full edition includes its base content offline. Check the release page for current Frostpass support and downloads.
