Hexfold

Discover Hexfold, a six-suit 3D card game from Drunk Yeti with a 78-card deck, AI opponents, and themed lounges.

Six suits. A whole new hand.

Cups and Shields join the deck in a six-suit twist on Texas Hold’em. Hexfold introduces a new set of hands and invites you to learn them around a 3D table with five AI regulars.

Official release & downloads ↗

Learn the house hands

A 78-card deck makes room for Quints, Hex, Triple Pairs, and Double Trio. Discover how the extra suits change a familiar card-game rhythm.

Find your table

Offline single-player tables and freezeout tournaments provide different ways to play. Distinct lounges coordinate tables, cards, lighting, and music.

Make yourself comfortable

Touch layouts, keyboard controls, graphics settings, and reduced motion offer ways to adjust the experience. Cosmetic rooms are separate from the core rules.

Choose your edition

The official release page lists the current preview, Lite demo, Full downloads, and account integrations. This is a practice-points game: there is no real-money wagering, and practice points have no monetary value.
