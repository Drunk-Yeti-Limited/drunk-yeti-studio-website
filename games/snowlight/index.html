Snowlight

Meet Snowlight, Drunk Yeti’s arcade snowboarding game with original riders, tricks, and two-player local split-screen racing.

Carve neon mountains.

Bright slopes, original riders, and one more run. Snowlight is an arcade snowboarding game built around carving, jumping, tricks, and racing a friend down the same mountain.

Official release & downloads ↗

Make every run yours

Launch tricks, chase stronger scores, and explore snow-covered courses with a cast of original characters.

Race side by side

Play solo or challenge a friend in two-player local split-screen. Keyboard and gamepad controls are supported in the desktop game.

A familiar face on the slope

Running Desktop Buddy on the same computer can unlock the Yeti rider and Frostfire trail in supported builds. This cosmetic bonus is separate from full-game access.

Choose your edition

The Lite demo includes the Neon course and two starting riders, Odonis and Schmuck. Full is the standalone offline edition. Optional sign-in supports account features; read the release page for current Frostpass integration and available platform downloads.
