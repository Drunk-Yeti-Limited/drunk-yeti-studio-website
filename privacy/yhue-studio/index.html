Drunk Yeti Studio privacy policies

YHUE Studio for iPad Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026. Drunk Yeti Limited publishes YHUE Studio for iPad.

Art and project files

The app uses your touch and Apple Pencil input to draw on your iPad. Your drawings and exports go to the location you choose in Files. If you choose iCloud Drive or another storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Drunk Yeti Limited does not receive your drawings or exports. You can remove saved files from the location where you stored them.

Accounts, analytics, and advertising

YHUE Studio for iPad does not require a studio account or transmit personal information to Drunk Yeti Limited. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

YHUE Pro purchases

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. The app checks the purchase entitlement through StoreKit to unlock the Pro export options. Drunk Yeti Limited does not receive your Apple Account credentials or payment card details through the app. See Apple’s privacy policy for how Apple handles its services.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. Email support@drunkyetistudio.com with questions about privacy or to request deletion of information you sent to support.
