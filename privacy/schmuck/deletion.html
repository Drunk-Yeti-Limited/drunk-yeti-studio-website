Schmuck Deluxe: delete your data

You can request deletion without opening or reinstalling the game.

  1. Email drunkyetistudio@gmail.com from your account email address.
  2. Say whether you want only your Schmuck Deluxe data deleted, or your shared Drunk Yeti account and its linked data.
  3. We will verify account ownership and confirm how active games, pending purchases and any retained records will be handled.

Schmuck data includes saved game state, coin balances, reward claims and account-linked game records. Transaction/security records may be retained where needed for legal obligations, fraud prevention or disputes; we will explain any retention. Shared-account deletion also affects other Drunk Yeti services. Uninstalling does not itself remove server data. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or payment-card details.
