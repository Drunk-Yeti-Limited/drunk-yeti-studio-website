Schmuck Deluxe Privacy Policy

Drunk Yeti Game Studio · Updated 12 November 2026

Schmuck Deluxe is a multiplayer card game with optional saved virtual coins. Coins have no cash value, cash-out, player-transfer or prize-redemption feature.

Game and account data

Free practice runs locally. Preferences and local identity information remain on your device. Saved-coin play requires a Drunk Yeti account. Sign-in sends your email and password over HTTPS to our authentication provider, Supabase. Our backend stores account-linked wallet identifiers, balances, transaction records, daily reward claims, game actions and game state. These records let us restore saved play across devices, validate moves and prevent duplicate credits. Opponents receive public table information and their own cards, not your hidden hand.

Purchases and advertising

Google Play processes payments when purchases are enabled. We receive product IDs, purchase tokens and purchase status to verify transactions, handle pending purchases and refunds, and prevent duplicate credits. We do not receive your payment-card number. Purchase tokens are encrypted in our retry records. Android includes Google Mobile Ads and its consent component. Google's SDKs may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics under Google's privacy policy and applicable consent settings, including SDK activity when no ad appears. Rewarded ads are optional; availability depends on consent, region and inventory. Server reward verification uses a wallet-linked identifier, a one-use reward ticket and Google's signed transaction information.

Service providers and security

Supabase provides authentication, database hosting and backend functions. Google provides Play Billing, advertising and consent services. Network requests and server logs can include IP addresses, timestamps and technical information used to operate and protect the service. We use HTTPS and restrict account and wallet access. We do not request contacts, precise GPS location, camera or microphone access.

Retention and deletion

Saved data remains available while you use the service. You can request deletion of Schmuck data or your shared Drunk Yeti account through the link below or by emailing drunkyetistudio@gmail.com. Shared-account deletion affects other linked Drunk Yeti services. We verify ownership and may need to resolve active games or pending transactions. Records needed for legal obligations, security or disputes may be retained for those purposes, with an explanation when handling your request. Uninstalling the app does not delete server data; clearing local storage removes local preferences and practice data.

Your choices

Free practice does not require an account. Use the game's ad privacy controls to manage available advertising choices. Optional videos and purchases are not required for free practice. The saved-coin service is intended for adults. For privacy questions, contact drunkyetistudio@gmail.com.
