Hexfold Hold’em Privacy Policy

Drunk Yeti Game Studio · Updated 12 November 2026

Hexfold is an adult, simulated poker game. Virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for money or prizes.

Data used to provide the game

Free practice saves settings, hands and statistics on your device. Online play sends a guest or account identifier, game actions, coin balances, reward claims and transaction history to our hosted backend. Guest identities are stored on your device; Android protects the guest secret using its Keystore. Optional account sign-in sends your email and password over HTTPS to our authentication provider, Supabase. Account-linked unlocks and statistics let you use supported Drunk Yeti services across devices.

Purchases and advertising

When Google Play checkout is enabled, Google processes payment details. We receive product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase status to validate purchases, restore entitlements and prevent duplicate or fraudulent credits. We do not receive your payment-card number. Google Mobile Ads and its consent component are included in the Android app. Google’s SDKs may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic information under Google’s privacy policy and applicable consent settings. SDK data handling must be considered even when no ad is shown. Reward verification uses a wallet-linked identifier and a one-use reward nonce. Ad availability depends on consent, your region and ad inventory. Google Play purchases are available only when checkout is enabled for your installed version.

Providers and security

Supabase hosts authentication, saved game data and backend functions. Google provides Play Billing and, where enabled, advertising and consent services. Network requests and server logs can include IP addresses, timestamps and technical request information for operation and security. We use HTTPS for network communication and restrict access to account and wallet data. We do not ask for contacts, precise GPS location, camera or microphone access.

Retention and deletion

Saved account and wallet data remains available while you use these services. You can request deletion of Hexfold data or your shared Drunk Yeti account using the button below or by emailing drunkyetistudio@gmail.com. Specify which you want deleted. Deleting a shared Drunk Yeti account also affects its linked services. We verify ownership before deletion; do not send passwords, guest secrets or payment-card details. Active hands and pending transactions may need to be resolved first. Records needed for legal obligations, security or dispute resolution may be retained for those purposes; we will explain any such retention when handling your request. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete server data. Removing the app’s local storage deletes local practice data and may make an unlinked guest wallet inaccessible.

Your choices and contact

Account sign-in is optional. Free practice can be used without a saved wallet. Where ads are enabled, privacy choices are available through the game’s ad privacy controls. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact drunkyetistudio@gmail.com. This game is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
