Hexfold account & data deletion

Drunk Yeti Game Studio

Email drunkyetistudio@gmail.com from your account email address. State whether you want to delete Hexfold data only or your entire shared Drunk Yeti account. You can also start this request in Hexfold: Settings → Privacy & account deletion.

We verify ownership before processing. Never send your password, guest secret or payment-card information. For a guest wallet, include its wallet ID if available; we may need further ownership verification. Active hands and pending transactions may need resolution first.

Deletion removes the associated Hexfold wallet, saved balance, game records and account-linked Hexfold data. Deleting the entire shared account also affects other Drunk Yeti services. Records needed for legal obligations, security or dispute resolution may be retained for those purposes; any applicable retention will be explained when handling your request. Google’s own purchase records are managed by Google. Deletion does not automatically cancel store subscriptions; manage those with the store.
