Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 11, 2026

Drunk Yeti Studio operates A Rocket’s Intensity (also called Rockets Intensity or ARI). This policy covers the Android phone, tablet and Android TV versions, and the account and online-arena services they use. Other Drunk Yeti products may have separate policies.

The essentials

You can play the available offline guest worlds without an account. Signing in enables account-linked progress, unlocks and personal flight statistics. Online play shares your pilot name and gameplay with other players.

Rewarded videos and purchases are optional. The phone/tablet build includes Google’s advertising and consent tools; the Android TV build does not include the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Use Ad privacy choices in the app to revisit available advertising choices.

Privacy questions or requests: support@drunkyetistudio.com.

3. Advertising and your choices

Rewarded videos are offered in exchange for the reward shown before you choose to watch. You can decline a video and continue playing the content you already have. Completing a video must be verified before a permanent account reward is granted; closing it early may not earn a reward.

Where applicable, Google’s consent form lets you consent, refuse consent or manage purposes and partners. Use Ad privacy choices in the app to reopen the available form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device’s privacy settings. Consent requirements and the choices available depend on your region and device.

The advertising SDK can initialize and process technical information when advertising is permitted by the consent system, before you choose to watch a particular video. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all technical processing or all non-personalized ads. If ads cannot be served, optional video rewards may be unavailable.

We use your consent for processing that requires it. Other processing is necessary to provide requested account, purchase and multiplayer services, meet legal obligations, or protect and maintain the service, where applicable law permits those purposes. You may withdraw consent through the available controls; doing so does not undo processing already lawfully performed.

4. How long we keep information

Account progress, unlocks and personal flight records are retained while needed to provide your account and restore its features, until they are deleted following a verified request, or otherwise no longer needed. Reward and purchase-verification records may be retained as needed to maintain entitlements, prevent repeated credits, investigate fraud and satisfy legal or accounting requirements. We do not promise an automatic fixed-day purge of these records.

The arena keeps a rolling set of up to 1,000 best run records, with up to 100 displayed publicly. A guest leaderboard entry is not necessarily linked to your studio account and may remain until displaced or separately removed. Contact us with the pilot name and approximate run date if you want us to locate an entry.

Support and security records are retained as reasonably needed for their purpose and applicable obligations. Data may remain temporarily in provider backups until those backups expire under the provider’s retention process. Providers may also retain information they independently control under their own policies. Removing the app clears its local app data but does not by itself delete cloud records or cancel a subscription.

5. Account and data deletion

In the app, open Account → Request account deletion, or use our account-deletion request page. You can also email support@drunkyetistudio.com with the subject ARI privacy / deletion request.

Your Drunk Yeti account is shared across studio products. Deleting it can remove account-linked access, cloud progress and unlocks in other apps. If you only want ARI data removed, tell support that explicitly instead of requesting deletion of the entire shared account.

The request page submits a request for studio review and can restrict account access while it is pending. It does not instantly erase your information. After we verify and process a deletion request, we delete the associated account data covered by the request, except information we must retain for a legitimate legal, security or fraud-prevention reason. We will explain any applicable limitation when responding. Guest arena entries may require a separate pilot-name request because they are not linked to your account.

Cancel any recurring subscription separately through the service where you purchased it. Account deletion does not automatically cancel Google Play or other recurring billing. We may request enough information to verify ownership, but never your password.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, receive a copy of, delete or restrict use of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of targeted advertising and related sharing. Use the in-app advertising controls where available and contact support@drunkyetistudio.com for other requests or an appeal of our response. We will verify requests and respond as required by applicable law. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Signing out stops use of that local signed-in session; it does not delete your account. Guest play remains available for the offline content offered without an account. Features that depend on an account or an internet connection cannot operate without the information needed for those features.

7. Security and international processing

We use encrypted connections for account and verification services, access controls for account data, and Android Keystore-backed storage for local sign-in secrets. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Our providers operate in multiple countries, including the United States. Information may be processed outside the country where you live. Applicable protections depend on the service, provider and relevant law; provider privacy policies explain their international processing practices.

8. Children and family privacy

Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information without any required authorization should contact support@drunkyetistudio.com so we can investigate and address the information. The app’s age rating and available features are shown in its applicable store listing. This policy does not itself establish an age rating or parental consent.

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The date at the top identifies the current version. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for a material change.
