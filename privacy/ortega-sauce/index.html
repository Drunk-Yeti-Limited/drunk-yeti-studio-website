Ortega Sauce Privacy Policy

Updated November 13, 2026

This policy covers Ortega Sauce: Alexandria, also known as Ortega Sauce: Alexandria the Great, Android package com.drunkyeti.ortegasauce, provided by Drunk Yeti Limited / Drunk Yeti Studio. Contact support@drunkyetistudio.com with privacy questions.

Guest play and connected features

Guest play stores your career, outfits, preferences and progress on your device. Optional Drunk Yeti accounts support cloud saves, account wallets and restoration of account-linked purchases. Purchases and rewarded videos require connected services. Feature availability can differ between test and released builds.

Accounts and Google sign-in

Supabase provides authentication for Drunk Yeti accounts. We process your email address, account identifier and authentication information. If you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies basic account information, which may include your name, email address and profile image, to our authentication service. The game does not ask for your Google password. Email-and-password login sends your credentials to the authentication service over HTTPS; the game does not save your password. Android session credentials and pending sign-in state are encrypted using Android Keystore.

Progress, stats and account records

Connected services process account-linked career saves, gameplay statistics, wallet balances, owned items, purchase and reward records, and save revisions. We use these records to restore progress, handle conflicting saves, deliver purchases, prevent duplicate rewards and investigate abuse. The tour destinations, venues and passport stamps in your career describe fictional gameplay travel; they do not track your real-world location. Server infrastructure also processes IP addresses and request logs for operation and security.

Purchases and virtual currency

Google Play handles Android checkout. We process product identifiers, purchase tokens, order and purchase status, and account binding to verify payments, deliver items once, restore entitlements and handle refunds. We do not collect payment-card numbers or bank-account details. Optional Yeti shard exchanges use your studio account wallet when available. Coins, entry passes, outfits and shards used by this game cannot be cashed out.

Optional rewarded advertising

Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform support optional reward videos and privacy choices. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device or advertising identifiers, IP-based approximate location, app interactions, crash information and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The game sends an account identifier and a one-use reward identifier so the server can associate a verified completed video with the correct account. The game does not award a reward solely because an advertisement was opened.

Consent choices are requested where applicable. Use Video privacy options in the game's progress/settings screen to manage available advertising choices. You can decline a reward-video offer and continue with free gameplay activities. Ad availability and reward confirmation can be affected by connectivity, ad inventory and verification delays.

Service providers

Google and Supabase provide authentication, hosting, purchases, advertising and security services. Advertising partners may process information for their own purposes as described in their policies and your consent choices. See Google's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy.

Security and retention

Connections to account, purchase and reward services use HTTPS/TLS. Account records and cloud progress are retained to provide account services. Transaction, security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, purchase disputes and preventing abuse. Deleting the app does not delete your online account. Local saves can be removed through your device's app-data controls or uninstall behavior.

Deletion and other privacy requests

Visit Request account deletion, sign in to the account you want deleted, type DELETE and submit the request. This starts studio review; it is not instant erasure. Account access is restricted and newsletter enrollment stopped while the request is pending. Because Drunk Yeti accounts are shared, deletion can remove your profile, cloud saves and account-linked access across studio apps. Financial records may be retained where required. Cancel any recurring subscriptions separately.

For Ortega-only data deletion, access, correction, questions about retention or help submitting a request, contact support@drunkyetistudio.com. Do not email passwords or payment-card details. Available privacy rights depend on your location.

Your choices and policy updates

You can use guest play, sign out, choose whether to connect cloud saves, decline optional purchases and videos, and use Android's advertising-ID controls. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact support. We may update this policy when the game or connected services change; the date above identifies this revision.
