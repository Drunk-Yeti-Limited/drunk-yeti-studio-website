House of Wilds Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026

This policy describes data use in Drunk Yeti’s House of Wilds (Android package com.drunkyeti.houseofwilds), provided by Drunk Yeti Limited / Drunk Yeti Studio. Contact support@drunkyetistudio.com with privacy questions.

Guest play and optional online features

Guest play stores game progress and preferences on your device. Signing in, synchronizing progress, using account wallets, purchasing coins and watching reward videos involve online services. Local coins and account coins are separate balances. Android TV does not use the mobile reward-video SDK.

Account and gameplay information

When you create or sign into a studio account, we process your email address, account identifier and authentication information through Supabase. The app does not save your password. On supported Android builds, a refresh token is protected using Android Keystore to restore your session. Account-linked services process wallet balances, reward claims, game transactions, and progress that you choose to synchronize. We use these records to operate the game, restore progress, manage accounts, prevent duplicate rewards and investigate fraud. Servers also process network information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure these services.

Coin purchases

Google Play processes Android purchases. We process the product identifier, purchase token, purchase status and account binding to verify the purchase and grant coins once. Transaction and wallet records support recovery and fraud prevention. The game does not collect your payment-card number or bank account details. Google handles payment information under its own policies. Virtual coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn for money.

Reward videos and advertising

If you choose to request a reward video on a supported mobile device, Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform process information needed for advertising and consent. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share device or advertising identifiers, IP-based approximate location, app interactions, crash information and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The app sends an account identifier and a one-use reward identifier to bind a completed video to the correct account. Rewards are granted after server verification. Consent choices are requested where applicable; the Coin Shop provides ad privacy choices when Google makes them available. You can decline a reward-video offer and continue playing.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Supabase’s Privacy Policy. Service providers process information to deliver authentication, hosting, purchases, advertising and related security services. Advertising partners may process data for their own advertising purposes as described in their policies.

Security and retention

App connections to account, purchase and reward services use HTTPS/TLS. Account records and cloud progress are retained to provide your account services. Transaction, security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where needed for legal obligations, purchase disputes and preventing abuse. Deleting the app does not delete your online account. Local saves remain on your device until removed through the device’s app-data controls or uninstall behavior.

Request account deletion

Visit Request account deletion, sign in to the account you want deleted, type DELETE and submit the request. This requests studio review; it is not instant erasure. Account access is restricted and newsletter enrollment stopped while the request is pending. Deletion can remove your profile, cloud saves and account-linked access across studio apps because the account is shared. Financial records may be retained where required. Cancel recurring subscriptions separately. For help or questions about retained records, email support@drunkyetistudio.com.

Your choices

You may play as a guest, sign out, choose whether to sync progress, decline optional purchases and reward videos, and use Android’s advertising ID controls. You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting support. Available rights depend on your location.

Audience and policy changes

This simulated casino game is not directed to children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact support. We may update this policy when the game or its services change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
