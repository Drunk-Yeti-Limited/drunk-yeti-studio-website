Nexus Wars Privacy Policy

Effective on the Android release date

Drunk Yeti Limited publishes Nexus Wars for Android. You can play without a Drunk Yeti account.

Data safety summary

Game data on your device

Match progress, preferences, and cosmetic selections stay on your device. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings to remove this data. Clearing local data may also remove your earned Ember backdrop.

Optional Google Play purchases

Google Play processes payments for the optional Aurora backdrop. The game receives purchase status and transaction details to validate, acknowledge, and restore that purchase. The game does not receive your payment card information. Google retains purchase records under its own policy. You can restore a purchase using the same Google Play account. Google Privacy Policy

Optional rewarded videos

Nexus Wars uses Google AdMob to provide an optional video that unlocks the Ember backdrop. Google may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Where applicable, Google’s consent form lets you make advertising choices. When required, you can revisit them under Cosmetics → Ad privacy options. How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Data safety

Game progress is stored locally. Google Play processes optional purchases. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share approximate location, device or other identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics. Google states that the SDK encrypts its collected data in transit. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

Privacy requests

For questions about data use or help with a privacy request, email Drunk Yeti Limited at drunkyetilimited@gmail.com.
