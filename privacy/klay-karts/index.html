Klay Karts Privacy Policy

Updated November 24, 2026

Drunk Yeti Limited publishes Klay Karts for Android. You can play without creating a Drunk Yeti account.

Data safety summary

Data stored on your device

Race progress, preferences, and cosmetic selections stay on your device. You can delete this data in Android settings by clearing the app’s storage. This may also remove the earned Sunset palette.

Optional purchases

Google Play processes payments for the Night palette. The game uses purchase status and transaction details from Google Play to verify, acknowledge, and restore the purchase. The game does not receive your payment card details. Google retains transaction records according to its privacy policy. Use the original Google Play account to restore your purchase.

Optional rewarded videos

AdMob provides optional videos that unlock the Sunset palette. Google may collect and share IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Google’s consent form lets you make advertising choices. Revisit these choices in Kart Paint under Ad privacy options when that entry is available. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Data safety

Klay Karts stores race progress locally. Google Play processes optional purchases. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share approximate location, device or other identifiers, app interactions, crash logs, and diagnostics and other performance data. Google states that the SDK encrypts this data in transit. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Privacy requests

For questions about data handling or help with a privacy request, email Drunk Yeti Limited at drunkyetilimited@gmail.com.
