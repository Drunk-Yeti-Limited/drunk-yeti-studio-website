Drunk Yeti Space Pinball Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026

Drunk Yeti Limited publishes Drunk Yeti Space Pinball for Android, iPhone, iPad, and Mac. You can play offline without an account. The game calculates your score and altitude on your device and keeps your personal bests in local storage.

Mobile advertising and consent

The Android, iPhone, and iPad versions use Google AdMob for ads between runs and optional videos that give you an extra ball. The game requests non-personalized ads. Google may collect your IP address and infer approximate location from it. Google may also collect device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

Google uses this data for ad delivery, measurement, service performance, and fraud prevention. The Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Google’s User Messaging Platform handles ad consent, and the in-game Privacy options button lets you revisit those choices where that option is required. The Mac game runs without mobile ads. How Google handles data from apps that use its services

Mobile purchases

Google Play or Apple processes any purchase to remove replay ads. The store sends purchase information to the game through Unity IAP so the game can enable or restore the feature. The game saves whether you own the feature on your device for offline play. The store provider manages payment details and transaction records. Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

Unity IAP may process purchase and transaction details, account or device identifiers, approximate location inferred from an IP address, and device, crash, and performance information. Unity uses this data to support purchases, prevent duplicate transaction processing, and measure how its service works. Unity Privacy Policy

Data retention and privacy requests

Personal bests and the local purchase setting stay on your device until you clear the game’s stored data. Clearing app data on Android or deleting the app and its data on iPhone or iPad removes those local values. The store can restore an eligible purchase. Google and Apple describe how they retain and delete data in their privacy policies.

For privacy questions or help removing local data, contact Drunk Yeti Limited at support@drunkyetistudio.com. We use information you send to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.
