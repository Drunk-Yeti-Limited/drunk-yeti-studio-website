# Provider logo sources

Downloaded from official brand resources on 2026-11-09. Logos retain their original shapes, colors and aspect ratios and identify the associated sign-in service. Provider trademarks belong to their respective owners.

- Twitch: https://brand.twitch.com/uploads/Twitch-Brand.zip — purple Glitch SVG.
- X: https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit — linked `x-logo.zip` from `https://cdn.sanity.io/files/kjy2lb0k/production/3bb3c110d1f6fc7c759ea7fafa18c4d7e21f380c.zip?dl=x-logo.zip`; white `logo.svg` on black.
- GitHub: https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip — black Invertocat SVG.
- Discord: https://discord.com/branding — white symbol at `https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6257adef93867e50d84d30e2/66e3d7f4ef6498ac018f2c55_Symbol.svg`, on Blurple.
- Google: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines — `https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png`. The original PNG bytes are embedded unchanged in an SVG image wrapper for repository transport. Displayed on white with action text.

No logo-generation service or third-party approximation is used. Assets are served locally; loading the login page does not send image requests to these providers.

Microsoft: official pictogram from https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps (ms-symbollockup_mssymbol_19.svg).
