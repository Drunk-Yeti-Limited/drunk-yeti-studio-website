Wild Camping Adventure

Read the transcript
  1. At sunrise in the mountains, the Yeti raises a blue can. “New day! New trails! New drinks!” A sign points toward adventure.
  2. He hikes past a sign marked “Beautiful Lake,” singing about hiking, drinking, exploring and living.
  3. At the lake he sits on a stump and says, “This is perfect.”
  4. A raccoon grabs the backpack. “Hey! My backpack!” the Yeti calls. “Mission accomplished!” says the raccoon.
  5. The two meet again by a cooler. A sign picks “Great idea!” for a drinking contest. The Yeti raises his can. “Let’s do this!”
  6. They fall asleep beside the fire. A sign reads, “Good company anywhere.”

Trail Mix

Read the transcript
  1. “What’s in the trail mix?” the Yeti asks the raccoon.
  2. The raccoon mixes pebbles, a pine cone, and a trail sign into the bag.
  3. “Needs more trail,” says the Yeti. The raccoon offers him a boot.

The Shortcut

Read the transcript
  1. “I know a shortcut,” the Yeti says at a fork.
  2. The two trudge up switchbacks past a mountain goat.
  3. They return to the same sign after dark. “We cut out the scenery,” says the Yeti.

Fish Story

Read the transcript
  1. Fishing from a dock with the raccoon, the Yeti calls out, “I feel a big one!”
  2. He reels in an old boot.
  3. A tiny fish peeks out of the boot and says, “Knock first.”

Tent for Two

Read the transcript
  1. The Yeti unrolls a tiny tent. “It sleeps two.”
  2. He tries to squeeze inside while the raccoon watches.
  3. The tent only covers the Yeti’s blue feet. “Two feet,” says the raccoon.

Echo Chamber

Read the transcript
  1. “Who’s the king of the mountain?” the Yeti shouts over the canyon.
  2. The Yeti and raccoon wait in silence.
  3. An echo answers, “Please hold.”

The Ferry

Read the transcript
  1. A beaver in a captain’s hat says, “One stick each.”
  2. The Yeti offers a pine tree.
  3. As his passengers float away on a log, the beaver waves. “Keep the change.”

The Northern Lights

Read the transcript
  1. The raccoon points at the green aurora. “Nature’s happy hour,” says the Yeti.
  2. The Yeti lifts his can to the sky. “I’ll have what she’s having.”
  3. The raccoon hands him a snowball. “On the rocks. Nice,” says the Yeti.

Hot Springs

Read the transcript
  1. The Yeti finds a hot spring. “This one feels perfect.”
  2. He settles in. The raccoon spots carrots and onions in the water.
  3. A moose in an apron asks, “Could you scoot? I need to stir.”

Trail Cam

Read the transcript
  1. The raccoon spots a trail camera. “Act natural,” says the Yeti.
  2. The Yeti strikes a glamour pose. The raccoon facepalms.
  3. A ranger looks at the photo. “That’s our third model this week.”

Postcard Home

Read the transcript
  1. On the summit, the Yeti writes, “Wish you were here.”
  2. The card is for the blue cooler at the bottom of the mountain.
  3. At sunset, the Yeti reunites with the cooler. “He knows. He was there,” says the raccoon.
