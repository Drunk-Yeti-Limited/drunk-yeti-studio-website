Wild Camping Adventure · Drunk Yeti Webcomic

A lakeside hike goes sideways when a raccoon makes off with the Yeti’s backpack.

← All Drunk Yeti episodes

Read the transcript
  1. At sunrise in the mountains, the Yeti raises a blue can. “New day! New trails! New drinks!” A sign points toward adventure.
  2. He hikes past a sign marked “Beautiful Lake,” singing about hiking, drinking, exploring and living.
  3. At the lake he sits on a stump and says, “This is perfect.”
  4. A raccoon grabs the backpack. “Hey! My backpack!” the Yeti calls. “Mission accomplished!” says the raccoon.
  5. The two meet again by a cooler. A sign picks “Great idea!” for a drinking contest. The Yeti raises his can. “Let’s do this!”
  6. They fall asleep beside the fire. A sign reads, “Good company anywhere.”
