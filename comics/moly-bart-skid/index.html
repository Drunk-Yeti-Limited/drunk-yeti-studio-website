Moly, Bart & Skid

Meet Moly, Bart & Skid in The Heart Under the Mountain, an illustrated adventure about friendship, missing water, and an ancient machine.

Three friends. A world beneath the mountain.

When Reedbank’s spring runs dry, a green mole, a flying yellow bat, and a blue squid doctor follow the missing water underground. What begins as a local mystery leads them into a machine serving two valleys.

Official release & downloads ↗

The Heart Under the Mountain

The first volume follows the trio through broken bridges, a drowned archive, and the mountain’s mechanical heart. Saving their home means understanding how the old waterworks connect their world.

Everyone brings something

Moly’s acrobatics, Bart’s flight, and Skid’s underwater skill open different routes. Patience, trust, and teamwork matter as much as getting to the next chamber.

A story with room to grow

This volume tells a complete adventure while opening a route to a wider mystery. It begins a chronological graphic novel series within Drunk Yeti’s original worlds.
