Account & game support

Get help signing in, connecting social accounts, recovering a password and finding your Drunk Yeti purchases.

Sign in or recover your account

Open Your account and use your original sign-in method. For email accounts, choose Forgot password, enter your email and follow the recovery link. Check spam if the message is missing. Never send us your password or verification code.

Connect Apple or another social account

Sign in to your existing account first, then open Social accounts and choose Connect beside the provider. This helps keep purchases and progress together. Apple’s Hide My Email can provide a different address; avoid creating a second account for an existing profile.

Where are my downloads and purchases?

Store purchases and downloads are managed by the store where you bought them. Check that store’s purchase history. Shards and eligible Frostpass benefits appear in your Drunk Yeti account. Supported features vary by game and build; consult the game’s release page.

How do I change my username?

Open Profile in your account. Usernames use 3–24 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores and start with a letter. Display names can use up to 48 characters.

Manage privacy and email

You can update newsletter preferences in Profile, unsubscribe, export game data from Security or request account deletion.

Still need help?

Email support@drunkyetistudio.com with the game, device, app version and a description of the issue. For purchases, include the store and order reference. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or sign-in codes.
