About Drunk Yeti Studio

Independent games, creative software and original worlds from Drunk Yeti Studio.

Design with a technical mind.

Drunk Yeti brings games, creative software and original characters together. The studio explores arcade play, tools for making games, music and art, and a growing collection of original worlds.

Michael Lambert Jr creates under the signature 3ichael 7ambert. Explore the current releases and follow the studio for new work.

Explore what we make

Start with the games collection for arcade experiments, card games, racing and original adventures. Our creative software collection brings together game makers and tools for working with art, music and projects.

Creators can also browse asset packs, while players can explore mods, soundtracks and illustrated stories. Each collection links to official release pages so you can check the current downloads, supported platforms and purchase options.
