Meet Drunk Yeti Studio

Meet Drunk Yeti Studio: original games, creative software, comics, music, and worlds by Michael Lambert Jr, also known as 3ichael 7ambert.

Design with a technical mind.

Drunk Yeti is an independent game, software, and multimedia studio led by Michael Lambert Jr, who creates under the signature 3ichael 7ambert. We bring playful ideas into games, creative tools, illustrated stories, and music.

Our mission is to make memorable worlds to explore and useful tools that help other people build their own. The work moves between art and engineering: a character can inspire a game, a production problem can become a tool, and a soundtrack can give a world another life.
